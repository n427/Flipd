# Age Rating Worksheet

Flipd’s Terms require users to be at least 18 or the age of majority. In App Store Connect, answer the current questionnaire truthfully and use **Override to Higher Age Rating: 18+** when available so the store rating does not conflict with that contractual minimum.

## In-app controls and capabilities

- Parental Controls: No.
- Age Assurance: No government-ID or platform age-verification mechanism. USC email verification is identity/community access, not age assurance.
- User-Generated Content: Yes—listings, profiles, reviews, messages, photos, and videos.
- Messaging and Chat: Yes—private conversations between approved request participants.
- Advertising: No advertising in the current source.
- In-App Purchases: None.
- Unrestricted Web Access: No general-purpose embedded web browser.

## Content frequency guidance

The marketplace prohibits alcohol, tobacco/nicotine, drugs, weapons, sexual exploitation, harassment, academic cheating, counterfeit goods, and illegal or unsafe services. Users can still submit prohibited content before moderation removes it, so answer user-generated-content questions conservatively rather than assuming the policy makes exposure impossible.

- Profanity or Crude Humor: Infrequent potential through user content.
- Mature or Suggestive Themes: Infrequent potential through user content.
- Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References: Infrequent potential through policy-violating user content.
- Sexual Content or Nudity: Infrequent potential through policy-violating attachments/content.
- Violence: Infrequent potential through policy-violating user content.
- Gambling, Loot Boxes, Contests, Simulated Gambling: None in product functionality.
- Medical or Treatment Information: None in product functionality.

## Safety capabilities to disclose

- Users can report listings, profiles, and conversations.
- Users can block other users.
- Community Guidelines and Terms prohibit unsafe and abusive content.
- Flipd does not promise live monitoring.
