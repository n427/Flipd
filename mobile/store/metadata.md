## Description

Flipd is a marketplace for the USC community. Every account signs in with a verified @usc.edu email, helping students discover who is buying, selling, and creating around campus.

DISCOVER WHAT TROJANS OFFER

Browse furniture, books, housing, student services, food popups, events, and more. Search and filter recent listings, save what interests you, and see the student behind each post.

POST IN MINUTES

Add photos, choose a category, set a price or event time, and publish directly from your phone. Edit, archive, or remove your own listings whenever you need to.

CONNECT INSIDE FLIPD

Send a request to a seller, then continue in a private in-app conversation after approval. Share messages, photos, or short videos without publishing your email address to other users.

BUILT-IN SAFETY CONTROLS

Report listings, profiles, or conversations, block another user, and review the Community Guidelines from your profile. Flipd verifies USC email access; users remain responsible for inspecting items, arranging safe public meetups, and evaluating each transaction.

Flipd is an independent student marketplace and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Southern California.
